## 9. Sauna

**Temperature:** 25–40°C | **Hours:** 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM | Traditional Finnish | Aromatherapy | Private Sessions

### The Experience
Built from hand-selected Nordic spruce with a continuously heated kiuas. 80–90°C dry heat. Löyly (water over hot stones) infused with eucalyptus and birch essential oils.

| Spec | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Interior heat | 25–40°C |
| Ideal session | 15–20 min |
| Rounds recommended | 2–3 |

### Session Guide
1. Arrive 10 minutes early — towels and robes provided
2. Begin with a warm shower
3. Enter and let heat work for 15–20 minutes
4. Cool down with cold plunge or outdoor air
5. Repeat 2–3 rounds
6. Rehydrate with cold infused water provided

### Health Benefits
- Circulation Boost — Dilates blood vessels, improving cardiovascular health
- Deep Detox — Intense sweating flushes toxins, cleanses pores
- Stress Relief — Triggers endorphin release
- Immune Support — Stimulates white blood cell production
- Muscle Recovery — Loosens tight muscles, accelerates recovery
- Better Sleep — Cool-down signals deep sleep onset

## ABOUT PEAKS

### Our Story

Peaks Hotel is a Kenyan-owned hospitality destination located in Nanyuki at the foot of Mount Kenya.

Inspired by the beauty of Mount Kenya, the Aberdare Ranges and the Lolldaiga Hills, Peaks has grown into a destination where hospitality, nature, wellness, adventure and sustainability come together.

From business travellers and holidaymakers to conference delegates, families and international visitors, Peaks welcomes guests seeking comfort, value and memorable experiences.

### The Three Peaks

The name Peaks is inspired by three iconic landmarks that define our horizon and identity:

1. **Mount Kenya**
2. **The Aberdare Ranges**
3. **The Lolldaiga Hills**

Together they represent the beauty, adventure and natural heritage that make Nanyuki one of Kenya's most remarkable destinations.

### Our Philosophy

**Beauty and Taste, with Nature**

- **Beauty** is reflected in our surroundings, gardens, flowers, hospitality and attention to detail.
- **Taste** is reflected in our choices — our standards, service, quality, professionalism and commitment to excellence.
- **Nature** provides the setting in which all this takes place.

Together they define who we are.

## SUSTAINABILITY & NATURE

### Nature is Part of Who We Are

At Peaks Hotel, sustainability is not a programme — it is a way of life.

**Our Philosophy:** There is no away to throw waste away. Our responsibility is to reduce, reuse, recycle and recover resources wherever possible.

**Sustainability in Action:**
- Solar Energy Systems
- Biogas Production
- Wastewater Treatment and Reuse
- Indigenous Tree Planting
- Water Conservation
- Resource Recovery Programmes

**Closing the Loop** — All wastewater generated within the hotel is treated and safely reused to irrigate gardens, trees and landscaped areas, typically within eight hours of treatment.

**The Peaks Micro-Climate** — Through indigenous trees, flowers, gardens and environmentally conscious landscaping, Peaks Hotel has created a green sanctuary that provides natural shade, fresh air, birdlife and beautiful surroundings throughout the year.
